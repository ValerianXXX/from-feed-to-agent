# Decision Note: Roborock S8 Pro Ultra Installation Evaluation

## Feasibility Assessment
* **Front Clearance:** **Infeasible.** The manufacturer requires at least **0.7 m** of free space in front of the dock. The proposed alcove provides only 0.6 m.
* **Above Clearance:** **Feasible.** The manufacturer requires at least **0.5 m** of headroom. The proposed alcove provides 0.6 m, which is sufficient to lift out the bins.
* **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** **Incompatible.** The Roborock S8 Pro Ultra hardware only supports the **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi band and does not support 5 GHz networks.
* **Dirty-Water Maintenance:** **False Claim.** The standard dock requires manual emptying and periodic cleaning of the dirty-water tank to prevent odors and bacterial growth. Completely automated drainage requires a specific refill/drainage model or separate plumbing kit modification, not the unchanged default dock.

## Unresolved Conditions
* Potential compatibility or availability of an aftermarket or regional auto-refill/drainage hookup kit compatible with the S8 Pro Ultra.
* Ability to reconfigure the home's router settings to broadcast a separate or co-existing 2.4 GHz SSID.
