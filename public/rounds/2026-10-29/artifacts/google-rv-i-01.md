# ROBOROCK S8 PRO ULTRA - SUITABILITY DECISION NOTE

### 1. SPECIFICATION VS. ENVIRONMENT ASSESSMENT
*   **Front Clearance:** The environment provides **0.6 m**, which fails the official manufacturer requirement of at least **0.7 m (2.3 ft)** in front of the dock.
*   **Above Clearance:** The environment provides **0.6 m**. This meets the 0.5 m (1.6 ft) threshold listed in some official documentation, but fails the **0.8 m (2.6 ft)** vertical clearance specified in other versions of the manual necessary to fully open the tank lids and remove them upwards.
*   **Wi-Fi Band Support:** The robot hardware is strictly limited to **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi**. It does not support **5 GHz networks**. It will not connect to the home's 5 GHz-only router.
*   **Dirty-Water Maintenance:** The owner expects zero manual labor. However, the standard RockDock Ultra requires the dirty water tank to be manually unlatched, emptied, and rinsed to prevent mold and extreme odors. Biofilm also requires periodic manual scrubbing.

### 2. WHY AN AUTOMATIC DOCK DOES NOT SETTLE SUITABILITY
An automatic dock handles the extraction of dirty water *from the robot*, but it simply transfers that waste to a dock storage tank. It does not connect directly to household sewage line plumbing out-of-the-box. Without a dedicated plumbing integration kit, human intervention remains mandatory to dump the stale wastewater, wash out the tank sludge, and clean the dock's mesh filter tray.

### 3. UNRESOLVED CONDITIONS & BLOCKERS
*   **Blocker 1 (Network):** Incompatible Wi-Fi network. The router must be reconfigured to broadcast a 2.4 GHz SSID, or a separate access point must be installed.
*   **Blocker 2 (Space):** Insufficient frontal clear path for docking alignment and safe navigation maneuvers.
*   **Blocker 3 (User Preference):** Heavy contradiction to the owner's "no manual dirty-water work" policy.
