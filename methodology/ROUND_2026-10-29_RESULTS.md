Twenty-four task cells preserve the historical three-category, four-stage, two-task structure. Six cells were sampled: WA-I-01, WA-C-01, RV-I-01, RV-V-01, SW-I-01 and SW-S-01. The fact denominator covers four selected claims per task, not every statement in a response. Extra claims about apps, prices, bands, durability or failure probabilities were not comprehensively audited. Three separate criteria cover the decision, uncertainty and usable note. Relevant video titles are discovery only: no videos were opened or watched. This intentional scope difference prevents an overall platform ranking.

## Findings that can be inspected

- Both answer interfaces produced a readable decision note for every submitted task. This alone did not establish retained evidence, correctness or a saved file.
- Google RV-I-01 introduced an unverified alternative 0.8 m overhead requirement alongside the 0.5 m requirement in the frozen Roborock manual. Its real Markdown export preserved the ambiguity. The selected clearance claim is uncheckable and the uncertainty criterion fails. Google WA-C-01 also overstated a protection rating as dustproof; four selected facts being correct does not certify the surrounding language.
- Google RV-V-01 produced a downloaded file with the four required facts, but its manual link was a retailer mirror. ChatGPT SW-I-01 produced a downloaded file retaining the four selected facts and the frozen Apple guide. File byte counts and hashes are checked against their actual saved files.
- ChatGPT SW-S-01 stated that export was unavailable even though the preceding independent task produced one. Record the absence of an export in that attempt, rather than treating a model's claim as a general interface capability. A generic error alert was visible beside the complete answer; no retry was attempted.
- Some correct answers cited official regional or locale-free URLs instead of the exact frozen URL. Those links remain visible as surface evidence, but do not earn the exact-reference gate. This is a conservative measurement limitation, not proof that those sources are unreliable. A future round should freeze a reviewed equivalence map before collection; this round is not re-scored to improve results.

## Time, intervention, cost and review

ChatGPT RV-I-01 was captured after 135.320 seconds and SW-S-01 after 227.279 seconds. These exceed the 120-second capture target. The first included interface hydration repair; the second included collector work and a context handoff. Their content remains available, but completion within the time limit is unconfirmed. Of ChatGPT's three content/reference passes, only two have capture windows within the limit. Google's two passes are both within the limit. These windows include operator and collection overhead, not model-only latency; first-useful latency is unmeasured.

Four extra ChatGPT operator actions across three tasks repaired initial input/submission activation. They did not add repair prompts: each captured conversation contains one user research message and one answer. The TikTok preflight used one offered guest action before the age form appeared; its cost and timing are recorded once, not multiplied across six unavailable slots. Zero recorded extra actions on other tasks is not evidence of zero human effort: normal query entry, observation and manual coding remain required.

ChatGPT used an existing account in temporary-chat mode; completed interfaces showed Medium thinking effort. An initial preloader showed Instant. The precise model and build were undisclosed. Google AI Mode and YouTube were signed out in the Codex in-app desktop browser. Account settings were not changed. Attributable subscription or inference cost is unknown for every surface and is not recorded as zero.

One agent performed first-pass coding. There is no independent human second coder, agreement estimate or independent first-time-user study. The ledger supports separate coder vectors and adjudication; its hash chain establishes edit detection, not observer identity or factual truth.
